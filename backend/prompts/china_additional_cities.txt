This batch covers 30 named OSM city nodes in mainland China and Taiwan. Explain
the name of THAT location, not a similarly spelled city or district elsewhere.

literal_name.text MUST exactly equal one value in INPUT_JSON.names, with the
matching language tag. The selected Chinese original may end in 市; keep that
character in literal_name.text. For 臺中市 and 臺南市, preserve 臺 rather than
silently normalizing it to 台. Do not replace the original with a shortened
core name even if that core is what you explain in the translations.

Trace names transferred from an older town, river, mountain, person or other
namesake only as far as credible evidence permits. Hohhot, Lhasa and Ürümqi
derive from non-Chinese languages; verify the source-language reading and do
not treat their Chinese transcription characters as the etymology. More
generally, do not assume a modern Chinese character gloss proves the historic
naming motive. Distinct interpretations should be separate aligned meanings
only if each is supported; otherwise return uncertain.

Keep zh, en, ja, fr and es in the same interpretation order. Cite only sources
actually checked in the English note.
