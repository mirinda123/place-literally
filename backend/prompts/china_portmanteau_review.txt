This review batch contains Chinese province names formed by joining the first
characters of older place names. Preserve the attested naming relationship.
For these four names, the publishable meaning should identify the two source
places, not translate the isolated characters as if they were chosen to form
a descriptive phrase. Return one interpretation unless a distinct historical
naming derivation is actually documented. Do not create alternatives from
multiple dictionary senses of a character in a source city's name.

安徽省: 安庆府 + 徽州府 -> a province named from Anqing and Huizhou.
江苏省: 江宁府 + 苏州府 -> a province named from Jiangning and Suzhou.
福建省: 福州 + 建州 -> a province named from Fuzhou and Jianzhou.
甘肃省: 甘州 + 肃州 -> a province named from Ganzhou and Suzhou (the historical
Suzhou near Jiuquan, not the modern city in Jiangsu).
